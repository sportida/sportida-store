Measure first.

Measure the circumference of your thigh 4 inches (10 cm) above the kneecap. Keep the tape level and comfortably snug, then match your measurement to the chart.

Measure around your thigh

Use a flexible tape measure. Do not measure across the leg or pull the tape tight.
